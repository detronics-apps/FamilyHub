# Family Hub: what is to come

This list is filled in as we plan and discuss. Each item says what it is and why, its **status**,
and the **tasks** agreed so far.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Discussed | Talked about, not yet agreed |
| Agreed | Build it when its turn comes |
| Parked | Agreed, but waiting on something, which is named |

**How it works:**
- Check [REJECTED.md](REJECTED.md) before adding anything.
- When an item ships, it leaves this file and gets its [FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md) entry in the same
  commit. How it was built goes in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md).
- Highest priority comes first. The order is the owner's to change.

## Faith: topics with their verses, pages, Compare, and sharing

**Status:** Agreed 2026-10-27; waiting for the owner's go.

**Why:** the starting topics were too narrow. "Eternal security" had nowhere to put its verses. The
owner wants other people to build on their study: "I even later want you to go through all my stuff
and create it as like a starter pack for other people."

**Tasks:**
- **Topics with their verses.** A topic is a name plus its verses, added together. The starting
  topics are no different from the family's own: all can be renamed, edited and removed.
- **Pages.** A page is a journal entry. Any entry can be marked as a topic, and a topic can be linked
  to a page, or turned into one, at any time. Not every topic needs a page.
- **Compare.** A free tool for comparing words and their definitions, topics, ideas and
  translations, to help make sense of the Bible.
- **Sources.** The Bible is the main source. Any text can name who it came from. This is partly done:
  creators and videos on journal entries (v0.9.33).
- **Export and import** the Faith section, ticking exactly what goes, as a starter pack for others.
- **The default topic list** may be updated later. Nave's Topical Bible was discussed as a
  public-domain option.

## Homes: why you are buying, and "was it the right call?"

**Status:** Parked 2026-10-30, until the report and its graphs clearly answer the questions people
come with. That report work shipped in v0.9.25 to v0.9.31. It now waits on the owner's go.

**Why:** people buy for different reasons, and each needs different sums. The owner also wants to
check a home they already own: "I just want to see if and when it was the right decision to buy.
Same for renting."

**Tasks** (the full plan is in [docs/homes-reasons-plan.md](docs/homes-reasons-plan.md)):
- **"Why are you buying?" at the top,** with only the sections that reason needs:
  1. To move into, instead of the home we own now.
  2. To move into, instead of renting.
  3. To rent it out.
  4. Comparing several homes, or several rentals.
  5. To live in it and rent out part of it.
  6. To buy now and move in later.
  7. A home for a child at university.
  8. A holiday home, perhaps let short-term while you are away.
  9. To renovate and sell.
  10. A plot to build on.
- **To rent it out:** estimate a fair rent as an investment, beside the rent the owner plans to ask.
- **"Was it the right call?"** for a home owned or rented, and from when it became the right call.
