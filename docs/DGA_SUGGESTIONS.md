# Dietary Guidelines for Americans 2025-2030 - suggested changes for approval

Read 2026-10-26 (10-page edition, January 2026). Already built from it, without needing approval:
the age and stage warnings (first year, young children, teenagers, pregnancy, breastfeeding,
vegetarian and vegan diets, older adults - `js/guidance.js`), the sodium limits by age as tips,
"8 to 10 tries" for new foods, and full-fat dairy for young children.

The suggestions below change how the app weighs or recommends food, so they need your yes.
Each says what it would change and what to weigh up.

| # | Suggestion | What it would change | Worth knowing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Protein target 1.2-1.6 g per kg** for adults (DGA) | Meal Assistant and the spider charts would use 1.2 g/kg (the low end) instead of the National Academies RDA of 0.8 g/kg when a weight is recorded | Higher than the RDA, WHO and NHS figures; meals would look lower in protein and protein ideas would come up more often |
| 2 | **Daily food-group servings per person** (3 dairy, 3 veg, 2 fruit, 2-4 whole grains per 2,000 kcal, scaled to each person's energy) | A "servings guide" on each person's card, and the weekly analysis checking the planned week against it | Turns the week view closer to target-tracking, which the Meal Assistant spec avoids; could be shown as a guide only |
| 3 | **Added sugar: at most 10 g a meal; none under 4** | A warning on meals and plates over 10 g added sugar, and for children under 4 | USDA data mostly lacks an "added sugar" figure, so this would rely on ingredient words (sugar, syrup, jam) rather than amounts |
| 4 | **Sodium limits by age on plates** (1,200 / 1,500 / 1,800 / 2,300 mg) | Calorie Checker and planned meals show a person's plate as a share of their daily sodium limit | Accurate where ingredients are weighed; salt added "to taste" is often missing from recipes |
| 5 | **Prefer full-fat dairy** in Meal Assistant ideas for children | Dairy ideas would name full-fat options for under-11s | In line with DGA; differs from older low-fat advice |
| 6 | **Fermented foods for gut health** (maas, yoghurt, kefir, sauerkraut) | A "gut health" wish on people, and those foods favoured in ideas for them | Evidence for specific benefits is still developing - wording would stay cautious |
| 7 | **Saturated fat no more than 10% of calories** | The fats chart would show saturated fat as a share of a meal's calories, with a gentle note above 10% | A limit, not a target - shown as information only |
| 8 | **Avoid highly processed foods** | Meal Assistant would offer a swap when a meal contains packaged snacks, sugary drinks or processed meat | Needs a list of processed foods; risks feeling judgemental if overused |
| 9 | **Iodine for pregnancy** (DGA top priority with iron and folate) | Add iodine to the pregnancy priorities | USDA data has iodine for very few foods, so it would mostly show "not known" |
| 10 | **South African guidance alongside** | Mention South Africa's Food-Based Dietary Guidelines where they differ (they are the local ones for your family) | Would need the SA document to read first |

Note: this edition differs from earlier US guidance and from bodies such as the WHO and NHS in
places (protein, full-fat dairy, the framing of fats). The app currently follows the National
Academies reference values; switching any of them is your call.
