## Name and tagline

**Family Hub** — one warm place to plan, decide and get things done as a family.

## Short description (cards, app listings, about 50 words)

Family Hub is a free app for running a home together: chores, meals, shopping, money decisions,
parenting, faith, exercise, medical accounts and more. It opens in your browser on a laptop or
phone, needs no account, and keeps your family's information in your own Google Drive, never on
anyone else's server.

## Meta description (search results, under 160 characters)

Free family app for chores, meals, shopping, budgets, parenting and faith. No sign-up. Your data
stays in your own Google Drive. Works on laptop and phone.

## Page introduction (about 180 words)

Family life is full: meals to plan, a house to keep, money decisions that matter, children growing
through new stages, and very little time to think about any of it. Family Hub gives a household one
calm place to share the load.

It is built around how much time you actually have:
- **Plan**, when you can sit down and think.
- **Check**, for a quick look or to capture something before it is forgotten.
- **Do**, when you have twenty minutes and want to get things done.

Nothing piles up when a busy day gets away from you. A chore simply waits until it is done, and
there are no streaks to break.

Family Hub helps you think of what you might not have thought of. It lays out the options and shows
the real numbers, then leaves the decision with you. For a home, that means every cost, the
alternative compared fairly, and the questions to ask at a viewing. For parenting, it gives the
questions to agree on together before you need the answer.

There are no sign-ups, no adverts and no tracking. Your family's information lives in your own
Google Drive folder, shared between your laptops, and your phones stay in step with one tap.

## What it can do

### Around the house
- **Chores** by room, with sample chores to start from. Each chore shows when it is due and waits
  until it is done.
- **Done** in one tap, or **Start** a timer ring with your own music. After a few timed runs, the
  app suggests a realistic time for that chore.
- **How much time the house really takes**, per week and averaged to a day.
- **Tasks** for one-off jobs, repairs and bigger projects, with their steps.
- **One family shopping list.** It knows what you usually buy and sorts it by kind: food, cleaning,
  hygiene, medical. "At the shop" mode shows just the list to tick off.

### Meals
- **A cookbook of meals, recipes and your own ingredients** (like home-made mayo), with versions of
  one meal: sauces and fillings that go with burgers, pancakes, pizzas and more.
- **Starter meals** to change and make your own.
- **Paste a recipe in.** Family Hub matches each ingredient, amount and unit to its food database.
- **A meal planner:** gather meals, sort them into breakfast, lunch, supper and snacks, then drag
  them onto days.
- **Portions for each person**, and leftovers planned in.
- **Send to shopping.** Sending again adds only what is new.
- **What is in a food**, from about 13,600 foods. Explore them by colour, type and nutrient to find
  new ones.
- **Time in the kitchen** for each day of the week.

### Money and big decisions
- **Homes:** buying, owning or renting, compared fairly with a real alternative on the same money,
  with every buying and selling cost item by item.
- **Your bond as it really went:** extra payments, money taken out and rate changes. See what each
  one did to the end date and the interest.
- **"How much could the answer move?"** Best and worst cases are worked out again, and the app
  shows what changes the answer most.
- **"Can you carry it?"**, year by year, and future prices in today's money.
- **The whole decision for a home or a car, not only the money:**
  - what you can see online;
  - questions for the agent, seller or dealer;
  - a viewing checklist (mould, roofs, cracks, plumbing, electrics, accident damage);
  - how it felt;
  - the finances.
- **Print the questions or the checklist**, and compare homes on what matters to you.
- **Cars:** the true cost, and petrol against electric.
- **Job offers**, your children's future costs, and **one money plan** with every goal on a single
  timeline.
- Every graph answers one question and marks the moments that matter. Print, save as PDF or export
  to CSV.

### Parenting
- **Plan ahead, together, calmly.** Topics are questions you decide together. Each parent gives
  their own answer first, then you decide.
- **Decisions that apply for an age.** Sugar under one, screen time at four. They stay in force
  while a child is that age.
- **Be prepared:** your own fever plan, a medicine chart, and emergency numbers for where you live.
- **Activity ideas by age**, with setup time against how long they keep children busy. **Play now**
  helps you choose in seconds: spin a wheel, pick a surprise, or let the children choose.
- **Routines** the children tick off themselves: leaving the house, bedtime.
- **Screen time noticed, not judged**, and shows ranked on your own family's values.
- **Trackers** for a worry (fever, sleep, eating), with a summary to take to the doctor.
- **A planner** for every child's and parent's activities, with trips and planned dates alongside.

### Us, faith and family values
- **Us:** the decisions you make together, date ideas, and the goals you share.
- **Faith:** Bible reading plans, a journal of questions and reflections with the passages you
  found, your own topics (each one gathers the passages and questions you have logged), and a guide
  to every book (who wrote it, when, to whom and why).
- **Tickets** to raise a concern, an idea or a thank-you at the family meeting.
- **Principles:** your family's values and agreements, with the reasons behind them.
- **Decisions treated as experiments:** try something, look back, and adapt.

### Health, exercise and the family
- **Medical accounts:** what is owed, what was paid, medical aid and gap cover claims, follow-ups,
  a view of the SARS tax year, and where each invoice and proof of payment is kept. It is not a
  medical record.
- **Exercise:**
  - your own exercises and workouts, with the muscles each one works shown on a body figure;
  - "I have 25 minutes" shows what fits the time you have;
  - exercises with your children that use their real weight.
- **People:** birthdays set each person's stage of life. Heights and weights are kept over time.
- **Holidays:** big trips with budgets, and places nearby for a free day, tagged "great for kids"
  or "pet friendly".
- **Creators and videos:** link the people you learn from, on every topic and on each recipe,
  workout, journal entry or decision.

## Why families choose it

- **Free, with no sign-up.** Open the link and start.
- **Private by design.** There is no server, no adverts, no tracking and no cookies. Your data lives
  in your own Google Drive folder, or only in your browser if you prefer.
- **The whole family, on every device.** Laptops share the Drive folder. Phones get the latest and
  send changes back with one tap each, and two people's changes are both kept.
- **Your decisions stay yours.** Family Hub shows options, numbers and questions. It does not tell
  you what to do, and it is not financial or medical advice.
- **Warm and homey.** It is made for evenings, weekends and the kitchen counter, in light or dark.
- **Built for South African families**, with rand, SARS, medical aid and gap cover. It also offers
  metric or imperial units and your own emergency numbers.
- **Open source** (MIT licence), so others can build on it and add their own small apps.

## Get started

1. Open **https://detronics-apps.github.io/FamilyHub/**.
2. Add your family on the People page.
3. Optional: connect a folder in Google Drive for Desktop, so your laptops share everything and
   nothing is lost. Your phones can then follow along.
