# Family Hub: decided against

These ideas were considered and declined, each with its reason. Read this before adding anything
to [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md). If something here comes up again, raise it: "we decided against this on
(date) because…", and let the owner choose again.

A decision that says what the app stands for is also written up as a principle in
[FEATURES.md](FEATURES.md) › The core philosophy.

---

## How the app works

- **Google's Drive API, sign-in and a cloud project** (2026-10-25). It needs the owner registered as
  the publisher on every family's consent screen. "I don't want to be the publisher of this app."
  The family's own Drive folder is used instead. (D-013)
- **Phones syncing by themselves** (2026-10-27). A phone browser may open a Drive file only when the
  person picks it. Doing it unattended needs a sign-in to a registered Google app, which the rule
  above excludes. Get latest and Send back are one tap each instead. (D-064)
- **A Detronics logo and business look inside the app** (2026-10-25). "Nothing in the app should feel
  like it was bought by a business or owned by a business." Only the coffee button, the footer link
  and the licence were added later, at the owner's request (2026-11-02). (D-005, D-103)
- ~~**Simple / Advanced / Expert for the whole app**~~ (2026-10-25) - *reversed by the owner on
  2026-11-13 (D-121): one level for the whole app, in a bar under the top bar, foldable.*
- **A Health section** (2026-10-26). It was really two things, and became Meals and Exercise.
  (D-023)

## Wording

- **"Jot", "jot down", "jotting"** (2026-10-27). Say "write". (D-051)
- **"Check and note"** (2026-10-27). The modes are one word each: Plan · Check · Do. (D-051)
- **Explanations inside buttons** (2026-10-27). A button is a name of one or two words; the detail
  sits beside it. (D-050)
- **"Ahead" or "behind" on their own** (2026-10-30). Always say ahead of what. (D-086)

## Chores and time

- **Not now, Skip, Tomorrow, Next week and Move to a date** on chores (2026-10-28). Putting a chore
  off hid it on the days there was time for it. "It just should not exist." (D-080)
- **Chores with Done as the only button** (2026-10-27). That was a discussion, not a request. Start
  stays, because timing a job shows how long it really takes. (D-080)
- **Workouts given fixed days** (2026-11-03). A missed day must not pile up. An optional day may come
  later, but never required (BACKLOG.md). (D-110)
- **Achievements for finding features** (2026-11-11). The skill asks for them, but even friendly
  badges sit against the family's rule: notice, never judge. Feature tiles and the guide do the job.
  (D-115)
- **Streaks, badges, "missed", "behind" and scores for a child** (2026-11-02 to 2026-11-03). Notice,
  never judge. Life happens. (D-097, D-110)

## Meals

- **"Paste several lines at once"** while writing a recipe (2026-10-26). A CSV file does it better.
  Pasting a whole recipe arrived later as Import. (D-044, D-092)
- **"Plates"** as their own kind of thing (2026-10-26). Everything is an item in the Cookbook, made
  "for the family" or "for" one person. (D-043)
- **A meal plan fixed to Monday to Sunday** (2026-10-27). Plan the meals first, then put them on any
  days. A shop is any run of days. (D-053, D-056)
- **Each person rating meals 1 to 10** (2026-10-26). Each person's preference (favourite, likes,
  dislikes) covers it, and the family rating exists only once a meal is tried. (D-034)
- **Fifty separate entries for one meal** (2026-11-01). "It's one meal." Use versions, and fillings
  and sauces ticked onto the meals they go with. (D-093)

## Finance

- **Comparing a home with "nothing"** (2026-10-30), meaning keeping your money and living somewhere
  for free. It is the wrong comparison. Every home is measured against a real alternative. (D-086)
- **The "After the bond is paid off" tick** (2026-10-30). It showed nothing. On the same money, the
  instalment you no longer pay is counted automatically. (D-087)
- **A second, hidden investment return** (2026-10-30). One "Investments grow by" is used everywhere.
  (D-086)
- **Bond terms fixed at 20, 25 or 30 years** (2026-10-27). "My house, I bought over a 14-year period."
  Any length is allowed. (D-067)

## Parenting, health and the family

- **Medical records** (2026-11-02). They will not come. Medical keeps accounts and paperwork, plus a
  few notes per person, such as allergies. (D-103)
- **The app's own for and against, evidence levels and sources on parenting topics** (2026-11-03).
  Where people disagree, recommending either way risks a family choosing something because the app
  said so. Awareness, not direction. (D-107)
- **The vitamin D question, and flowcharts using the app's own thresholds** (2026-11-03). They assumed
  an answer. The parents write their own fever plan instead. (D-107)
- **Any suggestion not to see a doctor** (2026-11-02). If you are wondering whether to go, go.
- **Asking about babysitters, for families whose rule is "only us"** (2026-11-02). "We never even want
  to be asked if a babysitter will be needed." (D-102)
- **Check-ins and reminders in Us** (2026-11-02). They duplicated Tickets and Decisions. "The
  reminder one, I'm not convinced." (D-102)
- **Things kept hidden between the two of you in Us** (2026-11-02). "We are seen as an entire
  family, so I don't want anything to be like hidden from each other." (D-102)
- **Fixed 15, 30 and 60-minute choices in Play now** (2026-11-02). "I might only have 5 minutes." They
  became sliders. (D-099)

## Creators and videos

- **Embedding videos, or fetching anything from a creator's site** (2026-11-01). Plain links only,
  which keeps "nothing leaves the browser". (D-091)
- **Made-up example creators, even as placeholders** (2026-11-13). "Do not make up data or information.
  Like, don't create fake creators." Only real creators the owner follows, supplied by the owner. (D-118)
- **Real people as example creators** (2026-11-04). Examples name only the kind of creator to look
  for. (D-112)
- **The creators list on the People page** (2026-11-04). People is only the family's people. Creators
  live on each topic. (D-112)
